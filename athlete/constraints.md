## The fixed base

> 🔧 Which runs are **non-negotiable** — the ones that happen whatever else does,
> because they are woven into your week rather than added on top of it?
>
> Commuting home on foot is the classic example: it costs no extra time, so it
> survives a bad week. Declare those here, and add the rule that matters:
> **a base run is shortened, never cancelled.** It gives the coach a way to
> absorb a bad week without proposing rest days that would break the habit.
