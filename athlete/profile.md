## History of interruptions

> 🔧 The single most valuable section, and the one everyone skips.
> List the breaks of a week or more over the last few years, **with their cause**.
>
> The pattern usually surprises people: for most amateur runners, the thing that
> stops training is the calendar, not the body. If 90 % of your breaks are work
> trips and moving house rather than injuries, then your plan's main enemy is
> your diary — and the coach should build around that instead of around injury
> risk.
