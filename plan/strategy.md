## The rules that govern progression

> 🔧 These constrain every week the coach builds. Copy them from §9 of
> [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md), or write your own — but write them somewhere the
> coach reads, or it will invent its own each time.

- Volume progression ≤ {{WEEKLY_INCREASE}} % per week
- A down week (−30 %) every 3 to 4 weeks
- Two quality sessions per week, never on consecutive days
- One variable at a time: volume **or** intensity, never both
